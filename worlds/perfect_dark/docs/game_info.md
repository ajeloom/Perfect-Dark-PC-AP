# Perfect Dark Archipelago Game Info
On the default settings, the game randomizes missions, weapons, and devices into the item pool. The default goal is to complete Skedar Ruins after completing a set amount of missions. Before reaching the end, you must first complete objectives and missions to get items required to beat the game. Missions can be done in any order, but can only be accessed once you received the mission. There are also additional settings that can give you more locations.

## Features
- **Goal**
  - **Skedar Ruins:** The game ends after beating Skedar Ruins. There is an additional option to set the requirements to unlock the mission.
  - **Complete Missions:** The game ends after completing the set amount of missions.
  - **Complete Challenges:** The game ends after completing the set amount of challenges.
  - **Complete Both:** The game ends after completing the set amount of missions and challenges.
  <!-- - **Cheese Hunt:** Adds cheese to the item pool. Once you collected the amount you set for the goal, the game ends. -->
- **Mission Difficulties:** There are three difficulties: Agent, Special Agent, and Perfect Agent. When a difficulty is enabled, then there will be checks for completing objectives and missions for that difficulty.
- **Mission Logic:** There are four logic levels for missions: Normal, Veteran, Hard, and Perfect. The harder logics will expect you to complete objectives and missions with less items.

  - **Enabled (per difficulty):** Missions for each difficulty is an item. -->
- **Weapon Progression**
  - **Normal:** Every weapon, except for the classic weapons, are in the item pool. You can only use weapons in the mission that they are found in.
  - **All Guns:** Every weapon is in the item pool. All the weapons you have received will be in your inventory. This allows you to use weapons in missions that they don't appear in.
  - **Progressive Weapon:** Replaces each weapon with a Progressive Weapon. You will always receive weapons in the same order. During a mission or challenge, every weapon you have reached progressively will be in your inventory.
  - **Progressive One Gun:** Same as Progressive Weapon, but you will only have the current progressive weapon in your inventory.
  - **Progressive Types:** Splits weapons into five types: Pistols, SMGs, Rifles, Explosives, and Other. You will have the current progressive weapon for that type in your inventory.
- **Progressive Weapons In Challenges:** If true, your weapons will be in your inventory during challenges.
<!-- - **Starting Items:** Gives you items at the start, so that you have checks to do. -->
- **Master Key:** If true, combines key cards into one master key item for that mission area.
- **Challenges:** Adds the combat simulator challenges as locations. Challeges can be done in any order.
- **Excluded Challenges:** Sets which challenges will not be a location.
- **Challenge Logic:** There are three logic levels for challenges: Strict, Normal, and Hard. Each logic will change what weapons it expects you to have to beat a challenge.
- **Challenges in Item Pool:** If true, each challenge is an item and must be found before being accessible.
- **Shorter Challenges:** Shortens a challenge's time limit and score limit.
- **Weapon Training:** Adds the firing range medals as locations.
- **Device Training:** Adds the device training as locations.
- **Holotraining:** Adds the holotraining as locations.
- **Completion Cheat Unlocks:** Adds the cheats unlocked by completing missions as locations.
- **Timed Cheat Unlocks:** Adds the cheats unlocked by finishing a mission quickly as locations.
- **Weapon Cheat Unlocks:** Adds the cheats unlocked by getting gold medals in the firing range as locations.
- **Cheats in the Item Pool:** If true, each cheat will be an item in the item pool.
- **NPCs:** Adds five characters to the item pool: Carrington, Cassandra, Dr. Caroll, Jonathan, Elvis, and the President. Certain missions and objectives cannot be completed until you have the character.
- **Multiplayer Unlocks:** Adds the multiplayer unlockables from completing challenges as locations.
- **Pickupsanity:** Adds checks for picking up hidden weapons, ammo crates, and shields in missions. 
<!-- - **Glassanity:** Adds checks for destroying glass in missions or multiplayer levels. -->
- **Traps:** There are traps that will last for a short period of time or activate at the start of a mission/challenge.
  - **DK Mode Trap:** Turns on DK Mode.
  - **Small Jo Trap:** Turns the player small.
  - **Small Characters Trap:** Turns everyone else small.
  - **Slow Motion Trap:** Slows down the game.
  - **Enemy Rockets Trap:** Gives enemies a Rocket Launcher.
  - **Perfect Darkness Trap:** Turns the map dark.
  - **Fast Animations Trap:** Makes enemies move fast.
  - **Skedar Trap:** Spawns Skedars on the map.
  - **N-Bomb Trap:** Spawns an N-Bomb on the player.
  <!-- - **Cloak Trap:** Makes enemies cloaked. -->
- **Death Link:** If true, you will die when another player dies.
